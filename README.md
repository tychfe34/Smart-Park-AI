# Smart Park AI

**MBA capstone case study — AI-powered parking platform for college campuses**

[View the live case study →](https://tylerchaffee.github.io/smart-park-ai)

## Overview

Smart Park AI is a venture concept addressing a common campus pain point: students and staff losing time circling lots for open parking, while university operations teams rely on manual patrols and outdated permit systems. The platform pairs camera-based occupancy detection with a mobile app to predict open spots, route drivers directly to them, and automate enforcement for campus parking departments.

## Approach

- **Market sizing:** Isolated the campus-specific segment within the broader smart-parking market ($5.7B global, ~10.5% CAGR) to derive a realistic serviceable addressable market ($170M–$500M)
- **Unit economics:** Built a bottom-up revenue model per campus ($0.75/space/month SaaS + $0.25 per paid transaction), then rolled it into a 3-year pro forma income statement, cash flow, and balance sheet
- **Financial modeling:** Calculated break-even subscriber count, NPV, IRR, and payback period; stress-tested the model with best/base/worst-case revenue scenarios (±30%)
- **Competitive analysis:** Benchmarked against incumbent vendors (Genetec AutoVu, T2 Systems, FlashParking, ParkMobile) to identify a defensible positioning around campus-specific AI and flexible institutional pricing

**Tools used:** Python (forecasting), Excel (financial modeling), market research synthesis

## Key results

- Break-even at **4,283 subscribers** (or ~2,611 with one university licensing contract layered in)
- **$1.07M NPV** at a 25% discount rate
- **190% IRR**, ~2-year payback period
