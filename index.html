The Case

Campus parking is a solvable, high-friction problem

Drivers circle lots hunting for open spots, and campus operations teams still lean on manual patrols and legacy permit systems. Smart Park AI pairs camera-based occupancy detection with a mobile app to close that gap on both sides.

Problem

Inefficient, manual, and slow

Students and staff waste time circling lots and dealing with outdated kiosks, while parking departments still rely on manual enforcement and disconnected permit systems.

Solution

Camera-first AI, built for one vertical

An AI-powered platform purpose-built for campuses:

  • Real-time occupancy prediction from camera + edge/fog AI
  • Turn-by-turn routing to open spaces
  • In-app payment, permit management, and reservations
  • An admin dashboard for enforcement and analytics

Where the model turns profitable

At $4.99/month per subscriber, ~$2.50/month variable cost, and ~$10,667/month in fixed costs, the model needs 4,283 paying subscribers to cover costs.

Break-even = Fixed Costs / (Price − Variable Cost)
= $10,667 / ($4.99 − $2.50) = 4,283 subscribers

Layering in one $50K/year university licensing contract drops that threshold to roughly 2,611 subscribers — the model's built-in case for prioritizing institutional sales alongside the student app.

Key risks identified in the plan

  • Campus adoption slower than projected
  • Regulatory changes in data privacy (FERPA/CCPA, LPR governance)
  • Competition from established players (Genetec, T2 Systems)
  • Slow university budgeting and procurement cycles
